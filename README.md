Operations & Quality Analytics

## Background & Why I Built This Repository

For 2 years, I worked as a Quality Assurance (QA) Analyst in a customer support environment. My day-to-day job was straightforward: I listened to recorded customer calls, checked if agents followed company rules, entered their marks into ready-made Excel sheets, and forwarded those sheets to our reporting analyst.

At the time, I only saw my small piece of the process. I wanted to understand what the analyst did with our sheets afterward—how hundreds of daily audit files and call logs were cleaned, combined, and turned into reports for team leads and managers. 

I built this repository while learning Python, Excel formulas, SQL, Power BI, and Microsoft Fabric so I could take raw support data and do that full analysis myself.

## What Is Inside This Repository

* **01-python-audit-scripts** – Simple Python programs that calculate call audit scores automatically, remove dropped/short calls from raw CSV files, and convert client penalty amounts from USD to INR.
